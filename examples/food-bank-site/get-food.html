Get food

You can visit once a week. Each visit, you choose your own groceries, including fresh fruit, vegetables, milk and eggs.

What to bring

  • Your own bags, if you have them.
  • Nothing else. You don't need ID or proof of address.

Home delivery

If you are over 60 or can't leave home, we can bring groceries to you. Call 206-555-0142 to sign up.

See our hours and locations before you come.
